Onboarding call

0 of 6

  1. Permission to record the call

    “Are you happy for us to record this? It's only used to improve onboarding.”

    No recording. Take written notes and capture their exact words in step 5.

  2. Add first property

    They drive, you watch. Ask them to share their screen and add a real one.

  3. Book or log one real viewing

    A real one, upcoming or already done. No test data.

  4. Send first invite to a tenant

    One tenant or applicant, sent while we're still on the call.

  5. Ask: What nearly stopped you?

    Ask it word for word, then stay quiet. Write their words, not yours.

  6. Agree on next check-in date

    Agree a date before you hang up. “Next week” doesn't count.

Flag any step where they hesitated. Flags go into the friction log. Saved on this device.
