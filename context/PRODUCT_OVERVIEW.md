# PROPTII: Project Overview & Core Truths

> **Source of Truth** for all agents, contributors, and automation pipelines.
> Last Updated: 12 November 2026

---

## 1. Company & Product Identity

* **Parent Company:** Luxcity
* **Product:** Proptii
* **Market:** UK Property Technology (PropTech)
* **Target Audience:**
  * **Landlords:** Individual (2–10 properties, 35–55 yo) and Small Portfolio (11–100 properties, 40–60 yo).
  * **Tenants & Property Seekers:** UK renters, students, parents, international renters.
  * **Letting Agents:** Independent and SME letting agencies.
* **North Star:** *"From finding properties to understanding property."*
* **Core Brand Lines:**
  * *"All the Rentals, One Spot"* (discovery & aggregation)
  * *"Property decision intelligence"* (risk, affordability, compliance, structural transparency)

---

## 2. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

* **Tone:** Operator, not consultant. Practical, transparent, human, authoritative.
* **Strict Negative Constraints:**
  * **NO** empty AI buzzwords (e.g., "revolutionary cutting-edge AI synergy").
  * **NO** sci-fi/holographic HUDs, floating data visual tropes.
  * **BLOCKED STATS:** Never quote **41%** or **£2,700** in any copy or collateral until independently verified and sourced.
* **Visual Identity & Design Tokens:**
  * `Proptii Blue`: `#136C9E` (Primary brand blue)
  * `Orange`: `#DC5F12` (Accent)
  * `Light Blue`: `#E7F2FF` (Supporting soft background)
  * `Light Orange`: `#FFEFD4` (Warm supporting background)
  * `Deep Blue`: `#004B71` (Deep brand tone)
  * `Warm Neutral`: `#F7F7F5` (Backgrounds / editorial feel)
* **Typography:**
  * Display / Headlines: **Archivo**
  * Body / Copy: **Nunito Sans**
* **Mascot:** Scout ("The PropTech Pup", golden retriever / yellow lab) — approachable guide, kept professional.

---

## 3. Product Architecture (The 3 Pillars)

1. **Property Discovery:** AI-assisted search, cross-portal aggregation, lifestyle & commute matching.
2. **Property Operations:** Landlord desktop dashboard, viewing management, multi-section tenant referencing, contract management.
3. **Property Intelligence (The Moat):** Pre-offer insights, EPC data, planning constraints, Land Registry data, hidden cost breakdown.
